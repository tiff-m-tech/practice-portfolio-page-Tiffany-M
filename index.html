About Me...

Hi, I’m Tiffany, a front-end developer and former cake decorator. After 11 years in the culinary arts industry, I found that many of the skills I used every day, like creativity, attention to detail, planning, collaboration, and problem solving, also apply to software development. My interest in web development started with HTML and CSS in high school and grew through personal projects using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I later completed a Full Stack coding bootcamp and have continued building my skills through Udemy courses and personal projects.

I’m currently enrolled in LaunchCode and looking for a web development role where I can contribute to meaningful projects, collaborate with other developers, and continue growing as a programmer :)
